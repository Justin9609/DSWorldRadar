DSWorldRadar — credits and third-party notices

Original radar foundation: MnM Radar by paroxis.

DSWorldRadar is based on the original MnM Radar by paroxis, used with permission.
In the correspondence supplied by the maintainer, paroxis explained that he had
stopped playing the game and was no longer maintaining MnM Radar. He preferred
a separate DSWorldRadar release under the new maintainer's name, with clear
credit, rather than requiring players to install his unmaintained mod first.
He also permitted reuse of mnm_nodes.csv and mnm_ids.csv and offered source code.
This does not mean that source code has already been received or integrated.

DSWorldRadar packaging and continued maintenance: zouzx1996.
Please direct DSWorldRadar bug reports to the DSWorldRadar maintainer/page.
paroxis is not presented as the maintainer or support contact for these changes.

Current implementation:
This package includes the MnM Radar-derived native core. The author offered to
look into making the source public; it has been requested but not yet received.
The existing native integration still uses fixed-offset access and UI patches;
the source-level replacement requested in the correspondence is not complete.
